How priority is scored

Score = severity x 4 + road traffic x 3 + repeat reports x 2 (up to 5) + risk factors x 2. The highest score gets fixed first.

Critical is 22 or more (fix within 2 days), High is 14 to 21 (5 days), Moderate is below 14 (10 days). A report past its deadline is flagged Overdue. Each report goes to the ward department its pin falls in.
